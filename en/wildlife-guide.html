📸 Photography System Guide📸 摄影系统指南

The new camera is your primary tool for documenting wildlife. Taking high-quality photos of animals unlocks them in your journal and progresses your collection. Here's how the photography system works:全新的相机是你记录野生动物的主要工具。为动物拍摄高质量照片可以在日志中解锁它们并推进你的收集进度。以下是摄影系统的工作原理：

1
Equip the Camera装备相机
The camera is available in your tool wheel once you start the Wildlife Update. Select it like any other tool to enter photo mode.开始野生动物更新后，相机将出现在你的工具轮盘中。像选择其他工具一样选择它即可进入拍照模式。
2
Find and Approach Animals寻找并接近动物
Explore the biomes to find wildlife. Move slowly and stay downwind to avoid spooking skittish animals. Some species are more approachable than others.探索生物群系寻找野生动物。缓慢移动并保持在下风处以避免惊扰胆小的动物。某些物种比其他物种更容易接近。
3
Frame and Focus构图和对焦
Use zoom to get closer without disturbing the animal. Center the animal in your viewfinder and wait for the focus indicator to confirm a good shot.使用变焦功能在不惊扰动物的情况下靠得更近。将动物置于取景器中央，等待对焦指示器确认良好的拍摄效果。
4
Capture and Review拍摄和回顾
Take the shot! A successful photo adds the animal to your wildlife journal. Higher-quality photos (closer, centered, well-lit) give better journal entries and more collection progress.按下快门！成功的照片会将该动物添加到你的野生动物日志中。更高质量的照片（更近、居中、光线充足）会带来更好的日志条目和更多的收集进度。

💡 Pro Photography Tips💡 专业摄影技巧

  • Stay crouched when approaching animals — you're less likely to be detected.保持蹲姿接近动物——你更不容易被发现。
  • Use maximum zoom for skittish rare animals to avoid spooking them.对胆小的稀有动物使用最大变焦，避免惊吓到它们。
  • Golden hour lighting (dawn/dusk) gives the best photo quality ratings.黄金时段光线（黎明/黄昏）能获得最佳的照片质量评分。
  • Front-facing shots where the animal looks toward the camera score higher.动物朝向相机的正面拍摄得分更高。
  • Revisit areas — animal spawns cycle throughout the day and night.重访区域——动物的生成会随昼夜循环而变化。

💡 Tips for Completing Your Wildlife Journal💡 完成野生动物日志的技巧

  • Explore every biome thoroughly. With 34 species spread across 4 biomes, you'll need to visit each one multiple times. Don't just stick to the starting grasslands — the canyon, coast, and mountains each have unique animals you won't find anywhere else.彻底探索每个生物群系。34种物种分布在4个生物群系中，你需要多次访问每个群系。不要只停留在初始草原——峡谷、海岸和山脉各自拥有你在其他地方找不到的独特动物。
  • Visit at different times of day. Many animals are crepuscular (active at dawn and dusk) or nocturnal. The great horned owl, for example, only appears at night. Cycle through morning, midday, evening, and nighttime to maximize your discoveries.在不同的时间段访问。许多动物是晨昏性的（在黎明和黄昏活跃）或夜行性的。例如，大雕鸮只在夜间出现。在早晨、正午、傍晚和夜间之间循环以最大化你的发现。
  • Be patient and move slowly. Rare and legendary animals are easily spooked. Crouch, move slowly, and use your camera's zoom to photograph from a distance. Rushing in will cause them to flee before you can get a shot.保持耐心，缓慢移动。稀有和传说级动物很容易受到惊吓。蹲下、缓慢移动，并使用相机的变焦从远处拍摄。贸然靠近会导致它们在你按下快门前就逃走了。
  • Check the Wildlife Outpost quest board. Daily and weekly quests can point you toward specific animals you might be missing. Quests also reward you with camera upgrades and useful items.查看野生动物前哨站任务公告板。每日和每周任务可以指引你找到可能遗漏的特定动物。任务还会奖励你相机升级和有用的物品。
  • Bring a friend in co-op. Exploring with a friend in co-op mode makes wildlife spotting faster and more fun. Each player builds their own journal, but you can share locations and tips with each other as you discover animals together.带朋友一起联机。和朋友一起在联机模式中探索可以更快、更有趣地发现野生动物。每位玩家建立自己的日志，但你们可以在共同发现动物时互相分享位置和技巧。
  • Pay attention to audio cues. Many animals make distinct sounds — bird calls, deer rustling, wolf howls. Use your ears to locate animals you might not see immediately. Headphones or good speakers can help a lot!注意声音提示。许多动物会发出独特的声音——鸟鸣、鹿的沙沙声、狼嚎。用你的耳朵定位你可能无法立即看到的动物。耳机或好的音箱会有很大帮助！

❓ Frequently Asked Questions❓ 常见问题

Is the Wildlife Update free?野生动物更新是免费的吗？

Yes! The Wildlife Update (v1.2.0) is a completely free major content update. It is not a paid DLC. All players get access automatically when they update their game.是的！野生动物更新（v1.2.0）是一次完全免费的大型内容更新。它不是付费DLC。所有玩家在更新游戏后都会自动获得访问权限。

How many animals are there in total?总共有多少种动物？

The Wildlife Update introduces 34 unique animal species distributed across all 4 biomes (Grasslands, Canyon, Coast, and Mountain). Animals range from common species like deer and rabbits to rare and legendary creatures.野生动物更新引入了34种独特的动物物种，分布在全部4个生物群系（草原、峡谷、海岸和山脉）中。动物种类从鹿和兔子等常见物种到稀有和传说生物不等。

Do I need to buy a DLC for the Wildlife Update?我需要购买DLC才能玩野生动物更新吗？

No. The Wildlife Update is a free game update (v1.2.0), not paid DLC. Simply update your game to the latest version and all wildlife content will be available to you at no extra cost.不需要。野生动物更新是免费游戏更新（v1.2.0），不是付费DLC。只需将游戏更新到最新版本，所有野生动物内容都将免费提供给你。

Can I take photos of my pet dog?我可以给我的宠物狗拍照吗？

You can definitely take photos of your pet dog with the camera! However, your pet dog does not count toward the wildlife journal collection — it's already your faithful companion, not a wild animal to discover. The 34 species in the journal are all wild animals found in the biomes.你当然可以用相机给你的宠物狗拍照！但是，你的宠物狗不计入野生动物日志收集——它已经是你忠实的伙伴，而不是需要发现的野生动物。日志中的34个物种都是在生物群系中发现的野生动物。

Does the Wildlife Update work in co-op?野生动物更新支持联机吗？

Yes! Wildlife photography and the journal system are fully supported in co-op mode with up to 4 players. Each player builds their own wildlife journal independently, but you can explore and discover animals together.支持！野生动物摄影和日志系统完全支持最多4名玩家的联机模式。每位玩家独立建立自己的野生动物日志，但你们可以一起探索和发现动物。

No, Outbound's wildlife is peaceful. Animals may run away if you get too close, but they won't attack you. The focus is on observation, photography, and appreciation of nature — not combat.不会，Outbound的野生动物都是和平的。如果你靠得太近，动物可能会逃跑，但它们不会攻击你。重点在于观察、摄影和欣赏自然——而不是战斗。
